The study

From sixty packages to a measurable reading of neutrality.

Kansei Engineering turns felt impressions into ratings. Twelve real packages were rated by 180 people on four bipolar scales, giving 2,154 valid respondent–package observations.

Phase 1

Select stimuli

60 personal-care, cosmetics and fragrance packages; five designers with 7+ years sorted them. 12 retained.

Phase 2

Build word pairs

30–40 terms from the literature, screened by experts and a pilot down to four bipolar pairs.

Phase 3

Survey two markets

Thailand n = 78 (51% non-binary or they/them) and Taiwan n = 102 (mostly binary-identified).

Phase 4

Translate to design

A composite configuration model, the individual-first GNS, principles and the T-INSPIRE prototype.

Measuring neutrality

A group mean can manufacture neutrality.

The Gender-Neutrality Score runs from 5 (both ratings at the poles) to 8 (both at the midpoint). Averaging ratings first hides disagreement, so this study scores each person first (I-GNS), then averages.

Cross-market results

A shared core, with local repositioning.

Overall neutrality did not differ between samples, but where packages sit did. Three of four semantic scales show a Country × Product interaction: a re-ordering, not a uniform shift.

Quadrant study · extends the thesis

Place the packages on Grohmann's two axes yourself.

The thesis measured gender on one bipolar scale, so the undifferentiated quadrant could only be recovered indirectly. This task measures masculine and feminine personality as two independent axes — directly — and asks which package reads as most neutral.
